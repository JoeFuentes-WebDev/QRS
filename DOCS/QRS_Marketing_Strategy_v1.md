# QRS Marketing Strategy (v1)

## Objective

Generate the first wave of active sellers through inexpensive,
measurable experiments rather than paid advertising or broad marketing
campaigns.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Core Product Philosophy

QRS is designed to eliminate unnecessary work for independent sellers.

Principles:

1.  Don't make the seller think.
    -   Infer information whenever possible.
    -   Avoid unnecessary forms and configuration.
2.  Don't make the seller work.
    -   Automate repetitive tasks.
    -   Generate content and assets automatically.
3.  Don't make the seller wait.
    -   The experience should feel immediate from signup through store
        creation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Target Customer

Primary audience:

-   People who primarily sell **in person**
-   Farmers markets
-   Craft fairs
-   Street fairs
-   Pop-up markets
-   Local artisan events

The product is **not** organized around what they make.

Whether they sell:

-   Pottery
-   Jewelry
-   Candles
-   Soap
-   Food
-   Woodworking
-   Art

...the workflow is nearly identical.

The market is defined by **how they sell**, not **what they sell**.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Primary Value Proposition

The easiest way for people who sell in person to start selling online.

The goal is not to overwhelm users with features.

The goal is to remove friction.

# Landing Page Goals

The landing page should accomplish one thing:

**Get the visitor to sign up.**

It should not attempt to explain every feature.

Detailed explanations belong in the FAQ.

The product itself should demonstrate its value.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Landing Page Content

The landing page should immediately answer:

-   Is this for me?
-   What do I get?
-   How long does it take?
-   Is there any cost?

The primary CTA should remain focused on creating a free store.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Marketing Experiments

Instead of large advertising campaigns:

-   Print postcards
-   Distribute them at local markets
-   Measure results
-   Iterate

Every experiment should produce measurable data.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# UTM Strategy

Each postcard campaign receives a unique QR code.

Examples:

-   ?utm_source=ferrybuilding
-   ?utm_source=alameda
-   ?utm_source=renegade

Optionally include:

-   utm_medium=postcard
-   utm_campaign=headline_a

This allows attribution without asking the user any questions.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Metrics to Track

-   QR scans
-   Landing page visits
-   Signups
-   Store creation completion
-   Stripe connection
-   First product uploaded
-   First order

Avoid asking users for information that can be inferred automatically.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Product Philosophy Reminder

Whenever considering a new feature, ask:

-   Does this remove friction?
-   Does this eliminate a decision?
-   Does this reduce work?
-   Can the system infer this instead of asking the seller?

If the answer is yes, it is likely aligned with the QRS vision.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Immediate Next Steps

1.  Finalize the landing page.
2.  Design and print marketing postcards.
3.  Generate unique QR codes with UTM parameters for each campaign.
4.  Instrument analytics for the full acquisition funnel.
5.  Distribute postcards at local markets.
6.  Analyze conversion data.
7.  Refine messaging based on observed behavior.
8.  Repeat.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Long-Term Goal

Build the simplest, lowest-friction platform for independent in-person
sellers to establish and operate an online storefront with minimal
effort.
